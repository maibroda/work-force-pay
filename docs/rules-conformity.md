## Architecture: what differs from the "intended" description

- **Stack.** React and TypeScript, yes, but as **Next.js 15 with server actions** rather than a separate Node API; **Prisma 6** as the database layer; PostgreSQL as intended. Docker is used for the database in development only. There is no deployment, and **no hosting region has been chosen** (the Lagos intention is not yet acted on).
- **Modular monolith.** Yes: pure rule libraries in `src/lib`, services in `src/server/services`, screens in `src/app`.
- **Payroll and statutory engines are pure functions.** Yes (`src/lib/payroll/engine.ts`, `paye.ts`), testable without a database, **but they use floating-point numbers** (rule 2).
- **Notifications by SMS.** Not built; there is email only.

## The product description, and where the work has gone beyond it

The rules describe the original product (workers deployed to client sites, a client representative who is read-only, support staff with no default access to salary data). Since then the work has followed your ThreeRoots payroll-bureau spec: **a client approver who approves or sends back each payroll in a portal, a client HR contact who submits inputs and employee changes, bureau staff who work inside many clients with access to their payroll, a sales CRM, pricing and proposals, and the payroll health check.** Those fit the ThreeRoots spec; some conflict with the rules' list of users. See the questions in the report.
