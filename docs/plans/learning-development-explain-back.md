This adds **a way to plan training and a link from attending a session to the certificate record that compliance reads**, so it touches the training compliance data: please read it. Built on your "go" of 2026-11-10 to the plan `docs/plans/learning-development-plan.md`. **You did not answer the plan's eight questions, so I used my recommended answers; they are listed under "Assumptions" below.** **How compliance is worked out does not change at all.**

## The rules, step by step

1. **A programme** is a course the company runs: a name, a provider, and how long its certificate stays valid in months (or never). **The name is the course name a certificate carries**, matched exactly as the compliance check matches it (not case-sensitive, spaces squeezed); so a programme named like a required course satisfies it. A programme is never renamed. The same course cannot be added twice, however it is typed.
2. **A session** is a dated occurrence of a programme: date (not in the past), an optional time, venue, trainer and **seats** (1 to 500). A switched-off programme takes no new session. The calendar shows a month at a time with seats taken.
3. **Enrolment.** HR enrols an **active** employee (or one on leave) of this company. **Seats are a hard limit**, checked while the session row is locked, so two enrolments at once cannot both take the last seat. Nobody is enrolled twice. A cancelled, completed or past session takes no enrolment. Taking someone off is only possible while they are still just enrolled.
4. **Attendance** is recorded by HR on or after the day of the session (never before; never for a cancelled one): **Attended**, or **Did not attend** with a reason. The employee never records their own.
5. **Attended creates the certificate record**, in the same step, **through the same function HR uses to record a certificate by hand** (`addTraining`): the course name, the provider, the **session date as the issue date**, and the **expiry = the session date plus the programme's validity in months** (none if it does not expire). The record carries a link to the enrolment that created it. **The compliance check then reads it like any other certificate**, so a Missing or Expired required course becomes Valid with no other change.
6. **A mistake can be corrected.** Changing Attended to Did not attend **revokes only the certificate that attendance created** (the existing "revoked" status, which compliance ignores), keeps it, and unlinks it. **A certificate HR recorded by hand is never touched.** Marking Attended again creates a new certificate. Marking the same result twice is refused, so there is never a second certificate for one attendance.
7. **A session is completed** once the day has come and everyone enrolled has been marked; it can be **cancelled** with a reason before it is completed (its enrolments stay but can no longer be marked).
8. **Needs.** A need records that a person needs a course and why (a compliance gap, an appraisal recommendation, a request, other), with a priority and a due date. **Added by a person, never automatically**; an employee cannot have two open needs for the same course. It becomes **Enrolled** when the person is put on a session of that course, **Open** again if they are taken off, **Met** when they attend, and can be **Dropped** with a reason; Met and Dropped are final. The page also **lists the compliance gaps that have no coming session** (computed from the existing check), with a one-click enrol onto a session of that course.
9. **An appraisal that recommended further training** does nothing by itself: HR names the course on the appraisal's "Training need" form, and only an approved appraisal with that recommendation can add one.
10. **The employee** sees only their own coming sessions and attended courses (with the certificate's expiry). No other person.
11. **Personal data and audit.** A person's enrolments (with the reason they did not attend) and needs (with their notes and drop reason) are in the copy of their data they can ask for; on erasure those words are removed and the shape stays. **Audit entries carry ids only**, never an absence reason, a need's note or a cancellation reason.

## A worked example

The company requires "First Aid" of everyone (valid 24 months, 30 days' grace for a new joiner). The Training needs page lists 12 guards **Missing** with no session. HR adds the programme "First Aid" (certificate valid 24 months), schedules a session for **20 November with 15 seats**, and uses **Enrol 20 Nov** against each guard; the page shows each as Enrolled. On 20 November HR marks 11 **Attended** and one **Did not attend** (on leave). Each of the 11 now holds a certificate "First Aid", issued **20 Nov 2026**, expiring **20 Nov 2028**, and the compliance view shows them **Valid**; the twelfth stays Missing and stays on the list for the next session. HR then realises one of the 11 was marked by mistake: changing them to Did not attend **revokes that certificate** (their First Aid goes back to Missing) and touches nothing else on their record.

## What could go wrong, and what proves it does not

| Risk | Test |
| --- | --- |
| A session is over-filled, or someone is enrolled twice | `training-plan.test.ts`: the next person is refused when seats run out; a freed seat is available again; a second enrolment is refused; inactive and other-company employees are refused |
| Attending creates no certificate, a wrong one, or two | the attendance tests: exactly one certificate with the right issue date and expiry (24 months, and none for a programme that does not expire); compliance turns Missing into Valid; marking the same result twice is refused |
| A correction damages a certificate HR recorded by hand | the correction test: only the attendance-created record is revoked and unlinked; a hand-recorded certificate for another course stays valid; re-marking Attended creates a new one |
| Attendance is recorded for a session that has not happened or was cancelled | the date and status tests (unit `canMarkAttendance` and integration) |
| A need is created or changed without a person | an approved appraisal that recommended training creates nothing until HR asks; the need's life is only Enrolled, Open, Met or Dropped, and Met and Dropped are final |
| The employee sees another person's training, or a cancelled session as coming up | the "my training" test: only their own, no other person's id in the result, cancelled sessions excluded, nothing without an employee record |
| A role that should not change training does, or another company sees it | the role tests: the auditor reads but cannot change; the employee is refused on every HR call; another company's sessions, needs, enrolments and appraisals are "not found" or empty |
| An absence reason or a note leaks into the audit log, survives an erasure, or is missing from the employee's data copy | the audit test (secret phrases absent), and the privacy tests (the data copy includes the employee's own and no one else's; erasure removes the words and keeps the shape and the audit log) |
| The migration loses data or changes compliance | round trip on a copy of the seeded database: 252 certificate records and 143 employees unchanged, none linked, schema identical; the compliance tests in the repository still pass |

## Assumptions (my recommended answers to the plan's questions; change any of them)

1. **Attending creates the certificate record**, with the programme's validity (HR can add the certificate number later). 2. **HR alone adds needs** in this step. 3. **No costs.** 4. **A hard seat limit, no waiting list.** 5. **An optional course** works the same way; it simply is not a required course, so it never appears as a compliance gap. 6. **A certificate from outside the company** stays as today (HR records it on the profile). 7. **No notifications.** 8. **The employee's page shows coming sessions and attended courses only**, not what they are required to hold.

Smaller choices you did not ask for: **correcting a mistaken attendance revokes the certificate** (keeps it, marked revoked), as the existing "revoke" does, rather than deleting it (the plan said "removes"); **seats count everyone enrolled in the session, including someone later marked Did not attend** (they held a seat); **enrolling is allowed on the day of the session**; and **a session can be completed only on or after its day**.
