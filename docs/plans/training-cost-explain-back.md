# Training cost and expenditure: explain-back note (slice 4A)

Written 2026-11-17 for the founder's approval of 2026-11-17 ("approve section 6 as recommended and go for 4A") under `docs/plans/hr-phases-4-5-plan.md`. **This is the first money in the HR area.** It **records**; it **posts nothing**: no journal, no payment, no link to payables. It does **not** touch pay, tax, statutory settings, payroll or bank files. One additive migration.

## What the code does, step by step

1. **Recording.** HR (anyone who can manage HR records) records a **cost line** against a training session: a **kind** (fee, travel, materials, venue, other; **never wages**, which is pay), an **amount in naira** to the kobo and above zero (at most ₦1,000,000,000, to catch a slipped decimal point), a description (3 to 200 characters) and an optional invoice reference (text only).
2. **The second person.** The company has a **threshold setting (empty by default: no second person)**. A line **at or above it** is saved **pending** and **does not count** until **someone else who holds the approval right** approves it; **the recorder cannot**. A decline needs a reason and the line never counts.
3. **Corrections.** A mistake is corrected by a **new negative line that reverses the old one, once** (the database enforces "once"); **nothing is ever edited**, so the history is kept. A reversal follows the same threshold rule.
4. **The budget.** HR sets a **budget for the year**: one for the company and, optionally, **one per department**. Setting it again replaces it; an empty amount removes it. **Over budget is a warning in the report, never a block** (recording is never refused for budget).
5. **The report** (anyone with HR view access: HR, finance, the auditor, the administrators). For a year: the **total of approved lines**, any amount **waiting for a second person** (not counted), the **company budget and what is left (or the overspend)**, and breakdowns **by programme, by month and by department**.
6. **How a session's cost is shared.** A session's approved cost is shared **equally per attendee** (those marked as attended), **to the exact kobo**: each attendee takes the whole share and the **kobo left over go one each to the first attendees**, so **the shares always add back to the exact total**. A department's figure is the sum of its attendees' shares. A session nobody attended (for example a cancelled one with a cancellation fee) counts in the total and is shown under no department.
7. **Privacy: no one person's cost can be read.** **No breakdown shows a row that comes from fewer than three attendances.** Rows that are too small are **combined into one "other" row**, and because that row would otherwise equal "the total minus the rows shown" (so a hidden row could be found by subtraction), **the smallest shown row joins it until it is itself large enough** (the same rule as the survey results). **Nothing in the report names or numbers a person.** A programme's **average per attendee** is shown only from three attendances.

## Worked example (the numbers the tests and the browser check use)

A fire-safety session has **six attendees** in this order: three in Operations, two in Dept B, one in Dept C. HR records a **₦300,000.00 fee** and **₦12,000.50 of materials**: **₦312,000.50 = 31,200,050 kobo**. Shared among six: 31,200,050 ÷ 6 = 5,200,008 each with **2 kobo left over**, so the **first two attendees take 5,200,009 and the other four 5,200,008** (sum: 31,200,050, exactly). Operations (attendees 1 to 3) gets 5,200,009 + 5,200,009 + 5,200,008 = **₦156,000.26**; Dept B (10,400,016) and Dept C (5,200,008) each have fewer than three attendances, so they are **combined into "other": ₦156,000.24**. ₦156,000.26 + ₦156,000.24 = **₦312,000.50**. The programme's average per attendee is 5,200,008.33 kobo, **half up: ₦52,000.08**. A second session of 4 attendees costing ₦90,000.00 gives ₦22,500.00 each. **With a threshold of ₦100,000**, a ₦150,000 venue line is **pending** until the Company Administrator (not the HR person who recorded it) approves it; until then the total stays ₦402,000.50 and **₦150,000.00 is shown as waiting**. With a **₦300,000 company budget** the year is "**over budget by ₦102,000.50**", a warning only.

## What could go wrong, and what I did not do

- **The first division in money rules.** Sharing a cost among attendees **cannot** round half up per share (the shares would not add back to the total). I used **exact sharing with the remainder going to the first attendees** instead, and said so here: **it is a departure from "round half up once" that you should confirm.** The only half-up rounding is the displayed average per attendee.
- **"First attendees" is the order of enrolment.** It moves at most a few kobo between departments; it cannot move a whole share.
- **Department figures follow the attendee's department today** (not at the session date). A person who moves department changes where an old session's share is reported.
- **A department with fewer than three attendances is folded away**, so a small company with only a few trained people may see "other" for everything. That is the cost of T5.
- **Not built:** wages or staff time (never), a link to the accounts or payables (on purpose), per-person training cost (on purpose), CSV export, and a currency other than naira.
- **The threshold is empty until you set a number:** until then **no second person is required** for any cost. Please say the number (or "none").
- **Not browser-checked:** the pages as finance, the auditor or a manager (the tests cover that they read the report and that managers and employees are refused, and CI's smoke test opens every page as every role).
