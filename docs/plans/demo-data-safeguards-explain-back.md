# Explain-back note: demo company safeguards

Decision 9 of the register (2026-11-14, "synthetic demo data approved, with safeguards"); the design was approved by the founder on 2026-11-19, with two later answers: **block** (not stamp) a demo company's bank file and remittance, and **drop the scoped reset** (demo data lives in its own database). Files: `prisma/migrations/20261224090000_organization_is_demo/` (migration and down.sql), `prisma/seed.ts`, `src/server/services/demo-guard.ts`, `payments.ts`, `payment-file.ts`, `src/app/(app)/layout.tsx`. Tests: `tests/integration/demo-company.test.ts` (9), `tests/integration/seed-synthetic-data.test.ts` (4), `tests/unit/demo-flag-writes.test.ts` (3).

**What it does, step by step**
1. **A flag.** Every company has a yes/no field, `isDemo`, that is "no" unless the seed says otherwise. The seed erases the whole database and rebuilds it, so at its very last step it marks every company in the database as a demo company. It is the only thing that ever sets the flag: a test reads all of `src/` and fails if any screen, action or service writes it.
2. **A banner.** On every page of a demo company, a yellow strip at the top reads "Demo company: every name and number here is invented, and nothing for this company can be paid or filed."
3. **A block.** Four things a real company does with real money are refused for a demo company, with that same plain wording: making a bank payment file, marking a payment batch paid, generating a statutory remittance, and marking one remitted. The check comes straight after the permission check, before anything is read, so a role without the right is still told "permission", not "demo".
4. **Reading is not blocked.** The preview of the file, the schedules and the reports still show on screen, so a demonstration still works up to the point where money would move.
5. **The seed refuses a real database.** This part already existed (`src/lib/seed-guard.ts`, tested): the seed will not run in production or against a database that is not on this machine unless the database's own name is typed as a confirmation.

**Worked example.** Someone signs in to Demo Security Services Ltd as Finance and opens the October payroll's bank file. The banner is at the top of every page. The download is refused: "This is a demo company, so a bank payment file is not available. Its numbers are invented, so they can never be paid or filed." The same person in a company that is not a demo company gets the ordinary file, or the ordinary reason it cannot be made yet.

**Where each rule comes from.** The founder's decision 9 (fictional only; clearly distinguished; isolated from production payroll and statutory calculations; a controlled reset) and the design in `host-configuration-and-demo-data.md`, section 3.

**What could go wrong, and what is NOT covered**
- **Someone runs the seed against a database holding real companies.** The seed guard stops production and any database not on this machine. A real company in a local database would still be erased: the demo data must live in its own database (see the dropped reset below).
- **A real company wrongly flagged as demo** would be blocked from its bank file. Only the seed sets the flag, so this needs someone to run the seed on the real database, which the seed guard forbids.
- **The scoped reset was NOT built (the founder's answer of 2026-11-19).** Deleting a demo company from a shared database was tried on a copy: about 50 database protections (posted journals, client invoices, tax records, bank payment-file records, billing rules, CRM history) stop rows being deleted, and switching them all off needs full database administrator rights. The demo environment is therefore its own database, wiped and re-seeded with the guarded seed.
- **Account numbers cannot be proved fictional.** The seed's bank account numbers are random ten-digit numbers; one could by chance be a real account. The test checks what can be checked (reserved test email domains, the synthetic tax ID and pension PIN formats, no eleven-digit identity-number lookalikes in text fields, ten digits for accounts). The block on the bank file is what stops a demo account number ever being paid.
- **Not blocked:** the schedules and reports on screen, and the creation of payment batches. They create no file and move no money.

**Tests that prove it.** `demo-company.test.ts`: all four actions are refused for a demo company with the plain wording; a company that is not demo gets the ordinary answer; one company's flag never affects another; permission is checked first. I confirmed they fail without the guard (five of nine failed with the guard switched off). `demo-flag-writes.test.ts`: nothing under `src/` writes the flag. `seed-synthetic-data.test.ts`: the seed's data looks fictional, as far as that can be tested. The banner and the real refusal were checked in the browser against the running application.

**Three existing tests were changed on purpose** (they generate a remittance on the seeded demo company): `business-lines`, `nhf-deduction` and `paye-by-state` now first switch that company to an ordinary company in the test database (`tests/helpers.ts`, `treatAsRealCompany`, with a dated comment). Their assertions are unchanged.
