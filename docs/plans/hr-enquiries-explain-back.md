# HR enquiries: explain-back note (slice 5A)

Written 2026-11-17 for the founder's approval of 2026-11-17 (section 6 of `hr-phases-4-5-plan.md`, rules E1 to E6 as recommended, and the choice to start slice 5A). **No money, pay or statutory setting is touched**: a pay question is a question, and nothing here can change a payslip. It is **its own pair of tables**, not the client Service Desk (which is a client company asking the bureau) and not an Employee relations case. One additive migration. **No automatic reply, no chatbot, no email.**

## What the code does, step by step

1. **An employee asks HR a question** (the right `enquiry.raise`: employees and supervisors). They choose a category (**E1:** payslip or pay question, leave, a letter request, a policy question, benefits, something else), give a short title and write the question. It gets a number (**ENQ-00001**, running on within the company) and waits for HR. The screen says plainly that **a grievance, harassment, whistleblowing or a welfare concern does not belong here** and links to "My cases and support".
2. **Who reads it (E3, E6).** Only **the employee who asked it** and **the HR administrator** (the new right `enquiry.respond`, held by the HR administrator alone). **Not** another employee, a manager, finance, payroll, the auditor, the Company Administrator or the platform administrator, and not the delegated HR officer. Anyone else who tries to open it is told it does not exist. An employee **never** sees anyone else's.
3. **Every read by HR is logged** in the record of who looked at sensitive details, **by the enquiry's own number and never by the employee**, once in ten minutes for the same person and enquiry. The employee reading their own enquiry is not logged. **HR's list shows the number, who asked, the category and the dates, never the subject or the words**; opening one is what is logged.
4. **The thread.** HR replies: the enquiry becomes **answered** and the **time of HR's first reply is kept** (only the first). The employee can reply again, which puts it **back to waiting for HR**, or close it. Messages are never edited. HR can close it **only after it has been answered** (so an enquiry is never closed unread: reply, or move it into a case). A closed enquiry takes no more messages: raise a new one.
5. **The first-response target (E4).** A company setting, **3 working days** unless the company changes it (1 to 30), in the HR policy settings. The due day is **the target in working days after the day it was raised**, counting **Monday to Friday and not the public holidays the company has entered**, on West Africa Time. An enquiry is **overdue once the due day has passed** with no reply (the due day itself is still on time, as with an acknowledgement). An answered or closed one is never overdue.
6. **The HR digest** gains a line for each overdue enquiry: **its number, the category and how many working days it has waited, with a link, and nothing else**. It never carries the subject, the words or the person's name, because the digest is an email.
7. **The sensitive route (E2).** On an enquiry's page, HR can **move it into an Employee relations case** (a grievance, harassment, whistleblowing or welfare case, never a disciplinary one). The case is opened through the **existing process** (its own rules and rights apply), **HR types the case's summary and description itself: the conversation is not copied**. The enquiry is then **closed and counted as answered**, and the employee sees a plain notice that it has been moved into the Employee relations process, **without the case number**. If the case cannot be opened, the enquiry is left exactly as it was.
8. **Erasure and the person's own copy (E5).** Erasing a former employee **clears the subject and every message** of their enquiries (what stays is the shape: that an enquiry existed, its number, category, status and dates). Their **own data copy** (a data access request) now includes their enquiries and HR's replies, and no one else's. **The retention period itself is for the lawyer:** no scheduled clear-out of old enquiries has been added (see below).

## Where each rule comes from

The founder's approval of section 6 of the plan, as recommended: **E1** categories, **E2** the warning on the screen plus moving into a case, **E3** the employee and the HR administrator only with every HR read logged, **E4** three working days as a company setting, **E5** cleared by the erasure of a former employee (the period is for the lawyer), **E6** never another person's.

## Worked example

Chioma (an employee) asks on **Tuesday 17 November**: category "A payslip or pay question", title "Is my overtime in my payslip", and her question. It becomes **ENQ-00001**, waiting for HR, **first reply due Friday 20 November** (3 working days after the 17th). The HR administrator's list shows "ENQ-00001, Chioma, payslip or pay question, 17 Nov, due 20 Nov", **no words**. HR opens it (**that read is logged as "an employee's enquiry to HR", ENQ-00001, not as Chioma**), replies on Wednesday, and it is **answered**; HR's first reply time is kept. Had nobody replied by **Monday 23 November**, the digest would say "ENQ-00001 (A payslip or pay question), raised 2026-11-17, waiting 4 working day(s)" and nothing more. Had Chioma's message been a harassment complaint, HR would **move it into a case**, writing the case's own summary, and Chioma would be told "HR has moved this into the Employee relations process" with no case number.

## What could go wrong, and what I did not do

- **Public holidays count only if the company has entered them** (Settings, public holidays). A company that has not entered them gets a slightly early "overdue", never a late one.
- **"The employee can reply once more" is built as "the employee can reply again, at any time until it is closed".** That is my reading of the plan; say if you want a limit.
- **HR can only close after replying.** An enquiry raised by mistake is closed by the employee, or HR replies ("raised in error") and closes it.
- **The case move is two steps, not one transaction** (the case is opened by the existing process, then the enquiry is closed). If the second step failed after the first, the case would exist and the enquiry would stay open; moving it again would then open a second case. I judged changing the case code for this not worth it; the case number is in the audit log either way.
- **Retention (E5) is partly done.** Erasure of a former employee clears the words. A **scheduled clear-out after a retention period** would mean a new category in the retention schedule, which waits on the lawyer's review of retention (retention is on hold). **Not built.**
- **No email to HR when an enquiry is raised** (the words about a person stay in the application); HR sees the count on its page and, once overdue, in the digest.
- **A pay question is answered by words only.** Nothing links an enquiry to a payslip or can change pay.
- **Not built:** attachments (they wait for the host and the encryption key), a tracked "waiting on the employee" clock, categories chosen by the company, and a report on response times.
- **Not browser-checked:** the pages as a manager, finance or the auditor (the tests and CI's smoke test cover the roles).
